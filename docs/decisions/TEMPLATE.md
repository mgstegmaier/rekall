# ADR-NNN: The decision, stated as a choice

## Summary

**Where it stands.** One or two sentences: the status in words, what is built, and what this is
waiting on.

### The problem

What is broken or missing, and what it costs, with the one or two numbers that prove it.

### The recommendation

What the author recommends (or, once decided, what was chosen), what it takes, and what it costs.

### Other options

| Option | Trade-off |
|---|---|
| B. | What it gains, and what it gives up. |
| C. Wait or do nothing | What waiting costs. |

### Decision needed

- **Name**: what they are choosing between.

## Detail

### The decision check (answer all five before ratifying)

1. Is the option of waiting or doing nothing on the table? State it.
2. Have the facts changed since the current plan was made? What changed?
3. Is every option priced, including hidden costs (warehouse time, rework,
   cliffs scheduled for later)?
4. Whose decision is this, and are they deciding it, or is momentum?
5. What check, on what date, tells us whether the decision was right?

### Context

What happened that forces a choice. Facts with sources, no adjectives.

### Options

| Option | Gains | Costs and risks | Priced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. | | | |
| B. | | | |
| C. Wait / do nothing | | | |

### Decision

What was chosen and why, in three sentences or fewer.

### Consequences and check

What we now own because of this choice, and the dated check from question 5.
